## Question

What is an exploration page and when do I create one?

## Synthesized answer

An exploration is the **crystallization** of a useful research conversation. The trigger is: you ask the LLM a research question, the LLM synthesizes 2+ sources to answer it, and the answer feels durable — not a one-off lookup, but something you'll want to reference again.

When that happens, the LLM offers (per Crystallization Protocol in `CLAUDE.md`): *"Want me to save this as an exploration?"* If you say yes, you get a permanent record of the question, the answer, and the sources it pulled from.

The point is to **stop re-deriving the same answers**. The first time you ask a question is exploration. The second time, the LLM should find your previous answer and cite it directly.
