# Decisions

> A decision log. Each decision gets its own dated file with the format below.

## Why decision logs matter

Conclusions become wrong over time, but **reasoning is forever**. Three months after a decision, the conclusion may be obsolete, but the reasoning will tell you exactly which assumption broke. That's the only way to learn from your decisions instead of just regretting them.

## File naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-<short-slug>.md`

Example: `2026-04-07-buy-EXAMPLE.md`

## Template

```markdown
---
title: <decision in 5 words>
type: decision
created: YYYY-MM-DD
related: [[entity-name]], [[concept-name]]
---

# <decision title>

## Decision

(One sentence: what you decided.)

## Context

(Two sentences: what you knew at the time, what was uncertain.)

## Reasoning

(The actual logic. Why this option, not the alternatives.)

## Trade-offs

(What you gave up to get what you got.)

## What would change my mind

(What evidence would make you reverse this decision.)

## Outcome (filled in later)

(Leave blank initially. Come back in 3-6 months and fill in.)
```
